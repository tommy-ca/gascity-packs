## Decisions

Panel children keep the step's existing `gc.run_target`. Arena candidates
stay `pstack.architect`. Interrogate review stays `pstack.reviewer`. Distinct
`gc.run_target` names plus `[[rigs.patches]]` rows freeze N in pack TOML.
That overlay is city-only. Reject it for pack formulas.

Dest standing remaining-units MUST contain the existing omit-panel strings
and MUST NOT contain `pstack.arena-runner`. Delivery evidence adds a live
FilePred over `pstack/formulas/*.formula.toml` and `gascity/`. `--root` still
relocates pack.toml, registry.toml, and `openspec/changes/` only.

## Risks / Trade-offs

A remaining-units sentence that names `pstack.arena-runner` even to forbid it
fails dest standing. Methods may name that string. Remaining-units forbids it
by absence.
